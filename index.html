Structural dynamics01
Individual projectUniversity of Windsor · 2025

Seismic performance of an RC frame with fluid viscous dampers

Modelled a three-storey, three-bay reinforced-concrete frame in ETABS and compared base-only and distributed damper layouts under Loma Prieta and Northridge records. MATLAB and Excel supported response processing and comparison.

18–20%typical Loma Prieta reductions
30–40%typical Northridge reductions

Decision The distributed 1,800 kN·s/m configuration gave the strongest overall response control in the model.

Life-cycle assessment02
Project lead & lead analystUniversity of Windsor · 2026

Recycled concrete base vs. virgin aggregate base

Led scope development, openLCA modelling, verification, sensitivity analysis, reporting, and team coordination for a cradle-to-site comparison of road-base alternatives over a 20-km corridor.

234.5 t CO₂e lower modelled impact for the recycled-concrete scenario—about 27.4% below the virgin-aggregate base case.

Screening-level study using European proxy datasets; conclusions were tested against transport, electricity, and material-loss assumptions.

Finite element analysis03
Two-person projectUniversity of Windsor · 2025

Cantilever-beam mesh sensitivity study

Built Abaqus plane-stress models to examine how element aspect ratio affects tip deflection and maximum bending stress. Compared five meshes with analytical beam-theory solutions.

2.95% deflection error with the near-square mesh; an aspect ratio of 3.6 gave the closest stress result at 1.39% error.
