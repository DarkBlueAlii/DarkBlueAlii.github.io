Seismic performance with fluid viscous dampers

Modelled a three-storey reinforced-concrete frame in ETABS under two earthquake records.

Engineering decision Distributed dampers with Ctotal = 1,800 kN·s/m produced the strongest overall response control.

Compared baseline, single-base, and distributed layouts at 600, 1,200, and 1,800 kN·s/m. Results are specific to the linear model, selected records, and scaling assumptions.

Recycled concrete base vs. virgin aggregate

03 · Finite element analysisTwo-person project · 2025

How mesh shape changes a cantilever result

Built five Abaqus plane-stress models and compared displacement and stress with analytical solutions.

Verification finding Near-square elements improved displacement convergence; AR 3.6 gave the closest stress result.

Evidence and limitations

Tip-displacement error decreased from 45.92% at AR 24 to 2.95% at AR 1.1. Results depend on the selected element formulation and boundary conditions.
